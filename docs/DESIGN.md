# PowerPick visual direction

The name's “Pick” becomes a practical pick list: a focused catalog, clear stock states, an editable basket and a legible order timeline. Forest green provides the main type/action field, pale neutral surfaces keep parts readable, and bright yellow points to the main action. System sans-serif type keeps the app fast and buildless.

The conceptual reference is [Leonxlnx/taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill): choose a subject-led direction, use real visual material, simplify the palette, keep hierarchy deliberate and inspect the browser result. No gradient hero, fake customer figures, fabricated testimonials, decorative dashboards or glass panels were added. Product cards and operational tables retain familiar shopping conventions. The repository's draw/inspector scripts were not installed or run; no formal taste-skill score is claimed.

## Generated assets

All three assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and copied into `public/assets/`. They are illustrative sample store assets, not photographs of verified inventory. Inline interface icons are small code-native SVGs.

- `brand-icon.png`: the original brand icon, reused directly as PNG favicon and Apple touch icon.
- `products.png`: a 4 × 3 catalog image sheet. CSS background positions select a product without extra image dependencies.
- `hero.png`: a single still-life image replacing the initial tiled hero composition after browser review.

## Final prompts

**Brand:** A professional PowerPick app icon for an electrical supply store. Bold geometric P merged with a negative-space lightning bolt; acid yellow #e3f451 on deep forest green #153d35. Tile fills the square edge to edge; balanced silhouette and padding, legible at favicon sizes. Flat colour, opaque background. No extra text, gradients, shadows, texture, border or mockup.

**Catalog:** A photoreal studio contact sheet, exactly four columns and three rows of equal square tiles on pale gray #f0f1ed. Row 1: red/black combination pliers, black copper cable coil, white single-pole MCB, white modular switch. Row 2: three white PVC conduit elbows, green/yellow earth-wire coil, black junction box, black nylon cable ties. Row 3: yellow general handling gloves, three brass cable glands, three copper ring lugs, black insulation-tape roll. One recognizable unbranded product per tile, generous whitespace, soft contact shadows, no text, logos or divider lines.

**Hero:** A single coherent product still life of a black copper house-wire coil, red/black combination pliers and white single-pole MCB on pale gray #eef0e9. Large coil behind, pliers across front left, breaker at right. Practical studio photograph, overhead three-quarter angle, all objects visible, soft contact shadows, horizontal 3:2 composition, no collage, grid, text, logos or extra objects.
