A few useful answers.

Where do you deliver? {{ icon('plus',17) }}

We deliver within India. Courier serviceability and delivery estimates depend on the delivery PIN code. See our shipping policy.

How much is delivery? {{ icon('plus',17) }}

Standard delivery is {{ shipping_fee|money }}. Delivery is free when your product subtotal reaches {{ shipping_threshold|money }}. The exact amount appears before you place the order.

How can I track my order? {{ icon('plus',17) }}

Open Your orders, choose an order, and check its timeline. Once dispatched, the store adds the carrier, tracking number and tracking link.

How do returns work? {{ icon('plus',17) }}

You can request a return from the order page within {{ return_window }} days of delivery. Tell us the reason and wait for approval and instructions before shipping anything back.

What is demo checkout? {{ icon('plus',17) }}

Demo checkout lets you try successful, declined and cancelled payment flows. It never charges money or requests real payment details. Demo orders are clearly marked.

Are prices inclusive of GST? {{ icon('plus',17) }}

Yes. Listed prices include the GST rate shown on each product page. The basket shows the included GST amount; it isn't added a second time.
