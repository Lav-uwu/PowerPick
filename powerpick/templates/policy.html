Standard delivery is {{ shipping_fee|money }} per order. Product subtotals of {{ shipping_threshold|money }} or more qualify for free delivery. Your checkout summary shows the final charge before you order.

Delivery address and serviceability

{% elif policy == 'returns' %}

If something isn't right, request a return from your order page within {{ return_window }} days of its recorded delivery date. For other product issues, contact support.

Request and approval

Describe whether the item arrived damaged, incorrect or unsuitable. Support reviews the request and provides the return address and instructions. Please wait for approval before sending items back.

Product condition

Keep the product, original accessories and packaging available. Tell us if an item has been installed, used, altered or cut to length, as this affects the available resolution. Fault reports are reviewed separately from change-of-mind requests.

Return delivery

Return shipping arrangements and any applicable charge are confirmed by support before collection or dispatch. Reported incorrect, missing or damaged items are investigated with the order details.

Cash-on-delivery refunds are arranged with support and recorded with a reference. Demo refunds are simulations and move no money.

{% elif policy == 'privacy' %}

PowerPick uses the information you provide to operate your account, fulfill orders and respond to support enquiries.

Information we store

Account names, email addresses, password hashes, order contents, delivery addresses, phone numbers, payment references, reviews and support messages are stored privately. Real card numbers, CVVs and UPI PINs are never collected by PowerPick.

How information is used

Delivery details are used to fulfill your order. Your email is used for order updates and support replies. Product reviews display your account name and review text. Payment information entered into a live gateway is handled by that provider under its own policy.

Session cookies

A signed session cookie remembers your sign-in and basket. It is necessary for shopping and checkout. This version of the store does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Access, corrections and deletion

Contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information. Some transaction records may need to be retained for operational or applicable legal requirements. The store will explain what can be removed.

Questions

Use our support form for privacy enquiries and include the email associated with your account. Never send a password or payment PIN.

{% elif policy == 'warranty' %}

Warranty coverage depends on the product and its manufacturer or supplier. A catalog illustration or product category does not establish a certification or warranty.

Before purchase

Check the listed specifications and ask support to confirm any warranty, certification or compatibility you require. Keep your order reference and any supplied warranty documents.

Report a fault

Contact support with the product, order reference and a description of the fault. Support will explain the available inspection, return or supplier warranty process. Do not attempt further use of a damaged electrical component.

Installation

Electrical products must be selected and installed by a suitably qualified person for the intended installation. General handling gloves are not electrical insulating PPE. Isolate circuits and follow the actual product's instructions and verified ratings.
